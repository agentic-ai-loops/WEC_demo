# Task 1: MCP prompts

Let's develop some server prompts.  They should be bundled as jinja2 templates in the source, and rendered dynamically to allow consistency whenever possible.

(1): documentation of the business schema.

There are typical questions an owner would like to ask about the business.  We want to create a MCP prompt that gives the overview of the business entities and their explanations.  These business entity descriptions are based on the graphql schema models (which in turn is based on the pydantic models), and must remain in-sync with the code.

In this documentation, we should refer to MCP resource discovery (tool and resource).

(2): task oriented skills as server prompts

the #owners are interested in their businesses, both at high level, as well as in detail, and they want to explore the entire business.  Somethings they are just trying to keep an eye on things, and other times, they want to get the specific data or information.  Other times, they want to focus on the concerns and problems.  They also want to update and mutate their business data.

Author one or more task oriented skills as prompts.

(3): Formatting guideline

- Encourage table oriented display since this is a business scenario.
- Provide a progress follow-up as to what the #owner can do next, and make a suggestion.
