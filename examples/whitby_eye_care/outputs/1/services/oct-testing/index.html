OCT Testing

Non-invasive scanning that shows the detailed layers of your retina and optic nerve.

Optical Coherence Tomography is an advanced non-invasive imaging test. This test allows your optometrist to visualize detailed layers of your retina and optic nerve. It allows for early detection of conditions such as glaucoma, macula degeneration, and diabetic eye disease. This testing service is offered in our office and is recommended for all adults and seniors.
